# Daniel J. Tyrrell, PhD

### Principal Investigator | Tyrrell Laboratory
**Heersink School of Medicine, University of Alabama at Birmingham**

---

## 🔬 Research Focus

Our lab investigates **aging-specific disease mechanisms** using advanced single-cell technologies. We model chronic inflammatory diseases in older mice to understand how age impacts disease pathology—distinguishing our work from traditional studies that rely on young animal models.

### Key Research Areas
- 🧠 Aging and neurodegeneration
- 🫀 Atherosclerosis development
- 🦠 Autoimmunity mechanisms
- 🔴 Cytotoxic CD8+ T cell contributions to disease
